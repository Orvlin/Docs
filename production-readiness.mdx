---
title: 'Production readiness'
description: 'What to verify before you send live traffic or real invoices through Orvlin'
---

Use this review before you move an integration from sandbox to a live workspace, and again after major changes to your pricing.

## Keys and access

- [ ] Sandbox and live use separate API keys, each stored in your secret manager.
- [ ] Each service uses the smallest [key role](/api-reference/api-keys#roles) that works. Services that only check access use `viewer`.
- [ ] Keys are rotated on a schedule, and you know how to revoke one quickly.
- [ ] Your team has the [audit log](/enterprise/audit-logs) and knows how to export it.

## Usage

- [ ] Every billable action produces exactly one event, sent after the action succeeds.
- [ ] Every event has an `idempotencyKey` derived from the action, not from the time you sent it.
- [ ] Each event's `eventName` matches the metric it counts. Check a sample with `GET /v1/events`.
- [ ] Rejected events are logged and retried after the cause is fixed.

## Entitlements and credits

- [ ] Checks run before paid work. Your code handles `hasAccess: false` with a clear response.
- [ ] You chose `onUnavailable` on purpose for each feature.
- [ ] Credit grants and purchases use idempotency keys.

## Webhooks

- [ ] The endpoint uses HTTPS and verifies signatures before parsing the body.
- [ ] Handlers are idempotent on the event `id`.
- [ ] Handlers respond quickly, and slow work runs from a queue.
- [ ] You've tested the endpoint with a test delivery.

## Billing

- [ ] A test clock in sandbox shows the expected first invoice, including usage and tax.
- [ ] Your plans, prices, and metrics have been reviewed against the [pricing models](/pricing-models).
- [ ] You know who can approve money-moving actions under your [approval policies](/enterprise/approvals).
- [ ] Accounting periods are closed on your close calendar, and reopening is restricted to admins.

## Operations

- [ ] Monitoring alerts on rising rates of `4xx` and `5xx` responses, and on webhook delivery failures.
- [ ] You know where delivery logs are: `GET /v1/webhooks/:id/deliveries`.
- [ ] Your on-call engineers have read the [reliability](/enterprise/reliability) page.
- [ ] You have a contact for incidents and know which details to include (workspace, request time in UTC, and request or event ID).

## Sign-off

Record who reviewed each section and the date. Keep the record with your change log, because auditors ask for it.
