title: 'Revenue reporting'
description: 'Recognize revenue over the service period and report it by contract, period, and waterfall'
---

Revenue reporting turns invoices into recognized revenue. Orvlin schedules how each invoice's revenue is earned over the period it covers, and gives you summaries for your books.

## Contracts and obligations

A contract groups the performance obligations it contains. Obligations are the parts of a contract whose revenue is recognized separately.

| Action | Endpoint |
|---|---|
| Create a contract | `POST /v1/revenue/contracts` |
| List contracts | `GET /v1/revenue/contracts` |
| Read a contract | `GET /v1/revenue/contracts/:id` |
| Update a contract | `PATCH /v1/revenue/contracts/:id` |
| Add an obligation | `POST /v1/revenue/contracts/:contractId/obligations` |
| List a contract's obligations | `GET /v1/revenue/contracts/:contractId/obligations` |
| Recognize an obligation as of a date | `POST /v1/revenue/obligations/:id/recognize` |

Recognition takes an `asOf` date. If you omit it, the date is today.

## Generate the schedule

Use `POST /v1/revenue/generate` to generate recognition schedules. See the [API reference](/api-reference/overview) for the request fields.

## Reports

| Report | Endpoint | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Summary | `GET /v1/revenue/summary` | Recognized and deferred revenue by period. |
| Waterfall | `GET /v1/revenue/waterfall` | How deferred revenue moves from period to period. |
| Contracts | `GET /v1/revenue/contracts` | Revenue for each contract. |

## Month-end close

1. Generate schedules for the month's invoices.
2. Review the summary and waterfall, and check them against your ledger.
3. Close the accounting period so the month can't change. See [Accounting periods](/enterprise/accounting-periods).
4. Export the audit log for the month with your close file.

<Note>
If an invoice is rerated after usage is backfilled, check the revenue summary again before you close the period. See [Backfill and rerate](/backfill-and-rerate).
</Note>
