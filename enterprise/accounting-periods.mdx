---
title: 'Accounting periods'
description: "Close a month so its invoices and usage can't change, and reopen it only with an admin's action"
---

When your finance team closes a month, close its accounting period. A closed period is frozen: Orvlin refuses any change that would alter what was billed in it, until an admin reopens the period.

## What a closed period blocks

| Action | Blocked in a closed period | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Usage events dated in the period (`POST /v1/events`, `POST /v1/events/backfill`) | Yes. The event is rejected with a reason. | Late usage would change the period's invoices. |
| Voiding an invoice (`POST /v1/invoices/:id/void`) | Yes, `409` | Changes the amount owed. |
| Issuing a credit note (`POST /v1/invoices/:id/credit-note`) | Yes, `409` | Changes the amount owed. |
| Approving a draft invoice (`POST /v1/invoices/:id/approve`) | Yes, `409` | Issues an invoice for the period. |
| Re-rating an invoice (`POST /v1/invoices/:id/rerate`, and `POST /v1/rerate` with `apply: true`) | Yes. Bulk re-rating reports the invoice as blocked and carries on with the rest. | Changes the invoice amounts. |

Paying an invoice from a closed period is **not** blocked. Collecting money already owed doesn't change what was billed.

A blocked request returns `409` with this body:

```json
{
  "error": "Accounting period 2026-09 is closed. Reopen it to make this change.",
  "code": "period_closed",
  "period": "2026-09"
}
```

## Close a period

Only workspace admins and owners can close a period.

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$ORVLIN_API/v1/accounting/periods/9/2026/lock" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN"
```

The path takes the month (`1` to `12`) and the year. Closing a period that has never been opened creates it, already closed. The response is the period, with `status: "closed"` and `closed_at`.

## Reopen a period

Only workspace admins and owners can reopen a period. Reopen only to make a correction, and record the reason in your own change log.

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$ORVLIN_API/v1/accounting/periods/9/2026/unlock" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN"
```

The response shows `status: "open"`. Reopening a period that doesn't exist returns `404`.

## Audit trail

Every close and reopen is recorded in the [audit log](/enterprise/audit-logs) as `accounting_period.close` or `accounting_period.reopen`, with the period and the person who made the change.

## Recommended close process

1. Confirm all usage for the month has been ingested. Events that arrive after the close are rejected, so chase late usage first.
2. Review the revenue summary with `GET /v1/revenue/summary`.
3. Close the period.
4. Export the audit log for the period, and store it with your close file.

<Tip>
Test the close in your sandbox workspace before the first real close. Try a void on a closed period to confirm the `409` appears.
</Tip>
