The audit log records changes in your workspace: who did what, to which resource, and when. Use it for investigations, access reviews, and compliance evidence.

## What is recorded

Changes to billing, catalog, customers, keys, webhooks, approvals, and privacy actions are recorded. Each entry includes:

- the **action**, such as `invoice.void`, `api_key.create`, or `credits.grant`
- the **resource type** and **resource ID** the action applied to
- the time it happened
- details of the change, where they apply

Examples of recorded actions include `api_key.create`, `api_key.revoke`, `webhook.create`, `webhook.delete`, `credits.grant`, `invoice.void`, and `invoice.credit_note`.

## Search the log

```bash
curl -s "$ORVLIN_API/v1/audit?action=api_key.*&since=2026-10-01T00:00:00Z&limit=100" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN" | jq
```

Filter by:

| Parameter | Description |
|---|---|
| `action` | An exact action name, or a prefix ending in `*`, such as `customer.*`. |
| `resourceType` | The kind of resource, such as `invoice`. |
| `resourceId` | One specific resource. |
| `since`, `until` | ISO 8601 bounds, inclusive. |
| `limit`, `offset` | Paging. Default 50 per page, maximum 1000. |

## Export

Add `format=csv` to download the matching entries as a CSV file for your records or SIEM:

```bash
curl -s "$ORVLIN_API/v1/audit?since=2026-10-01T00:00:00Z&format=csv" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN" -o audit-2026-10.csv
```

## Retention

Keep exports in your own storage, under your own retention policy. Confirm with your account team how long Orvlin keeps audit entries for your plan. If you need a longer period, export on a schedule.

<Warning>
Audit entries can't be edited or deleted through the API. Treat exports as evidence: store them somewhere write-protected.
</Warning>
