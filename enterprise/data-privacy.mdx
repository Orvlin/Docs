---
title: 'Data privacy'
description: 'Export or erase a customer's data to meet access and deletion requests'
---

Customers can ask what data you hold about them, and ask you to delete it. Orvlin gives you two operations for those requests.

## Export a customer's data

```bash
curl -s "$ORVLIN_API/v1/customers/cust_acme/data-export" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN" -o cust_acme-export.json
```

The export contains the data Orvlin stores about that customer. Save the file to your secure storage, and don't email it unencrypted.

## Erase a customer's personal data

```bash
curl -s -X POST "$ORVLIN_API/v1/customers/cust_acme/erase" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_SESSION_TOKEN"
```

Erasure removes the customer's personal information. Keep the following in mind:

- **Export first.** Treat erasure as permanent. Keep an export if you need one for your own records.
- **Check retention duties.** Some invoice and tax records may have to be kept by law. Confirm with your legal team before erasing customers with financial history.
- **The action is audited.** The erasure is recorded in the [audit log](/enterprise/audit-logs).

<Warning>
Confirm the request with the customer and your legal team before erasing, especially for customers with open invoices.
</Warning>

## Retention

Your retention settings control how long Orvlin keeps data that isn't tied to an erasure request. Read and set them with the privacy endpoints at `/v1/privacy/retention`.

## Responding to a request

1. Verify the requester's identity in your own system.
2. Export the data, and send it through your secure channel.
3. If they asked for deletion, erase the customer after confirming.
4. Record the request and your response in your compliance tracker.
